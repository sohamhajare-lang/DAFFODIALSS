Parents ask, we answer

When do admissions open?

Admissions open every year before the new session. Call or email us for the latest dates.

What is the school timing?

Monday to Saturday, 8:30 am to 3:30 pm. Nursery to UKG finish earlier.

Is school transport available?

Yes. Buses run on fixed routes across Belagavi. Ask the office for your area.

How can I see my child’s progress?

Log in to the Parent portal for attendance, results and notices.
